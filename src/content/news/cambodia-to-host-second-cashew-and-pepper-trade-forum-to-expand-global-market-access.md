\## Cambodia Prepares for International Trade Forum



Cambodia is preparing to host the second Cashew and Pepper Trade Forum from 10 to 12 November 2026 at Sofitel Phnom Penh Phokeethra. The event aims to strengthen international trade connections and promote Cambodia’s cashew and pepper products in global markets, with particular attention to Europe.



Organised through cooperation between the Royal Government of Cambodia, the European Union and Germany, the forum is expected to welcome approximately 300–400 participants, including farmers, processors, exporters, buyers, investors and business service providers.



The event will be held under the theme \*\*“Cambodia to the World,”\*\* with a special focus on trade with Europe.



\## Connecting Cambodian Businesses with International Buyers



One of the forum’s main activities will be structured business-to-business (B2B) matchmaking, allowing Cambodian producers and exporters to meet potential buyers, importers and investors.



The programme will also include discussions on international market requirements, export opportunities and investment partnerships. Participating companies will have opportunities to explore services related to processing equipment, product certification, logistics, packaging and financing.



These activities are designed to help Cambodian businesses build commercial relationships and improve their readiness to compete in international markets.



\## Strengthening Quality and Export Readiness



The forum will feature technical discussions and workshops on post-harvest handling, quality assurance, food processing and access to European markets.



These topics are particularly relevant to Cambodia’s cashew industry, where consistent raw material quality, reliable processing practices and compliance with buyer requirements are important for developing higher-value export opportunities.



The programme also includes planned field visits to farms and processing facilities, giving potential buyers and investors an opportunity to better understand Cambodia’s production and processing capacity.



\## Opportunities for Cambodia’s Cashew Sector



For Cambodia’s cashew industry, the forum offers an opportunity to strengthen connections between local processors and international markets.



Potential benefits include:



\- Building relationships with European and other international buyers.

\- Improving understanding of quality, food safety and export requirements.

\- Exploring investment in domestic cashew processing and value addition.

\- Connecting businesses with technology, logistics and financial service providers.

\- Promoting Cambodian cashew products as a competitive sourcing option.



These are potential opportunities rather than confirmed trade outcomes. Their long-term value will depend on partnerships and commercial agreements developed during and after the event.



\## Looking Ahead



The second Cashew and Pepper Trade Forum follows the inaugural event held in 2025. The 2026 edition places greater emphasis on practical business engagement and developing commercial partnerships.



As Cambodia seeks to diversify its agricultural export markets and increase domestic value addition, the forum provides a platform for producers, processors, exporters and investors to explore new business opportunities.
