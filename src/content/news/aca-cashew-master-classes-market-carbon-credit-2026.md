# African Cashew Alliance Announces November 2026 Master Classes on Market Intelligence and Carbon Credits

**Mato Cashew News & Events | 29 October 2026**

The African Cashew Alliance (ACA) announced on 27 October 2026 that it is launching its *Cashew Master Classes*, a professional capacity-building initiative for people working across the cashew industry. The first two courses are scheduled for November and early December in Côte d’Ivoire.

## Two courses announced

**1. Master Class in Cashew Market and Information System**

- **Dates:** 23–25 November 2026
- **Location:** Abidjan, Côte d’Ivoire
- **Instructor:** Jim Fitzpatrick
- **Fee:** USD 800 (or CFA 400,000) per participant

This course focuses on understanding cashew markets and the role of market information systems in industry decision-making.

**2. Master Class in Carbon Credit as a Green Fund in the Cashew Industry**

- **Dates:** 30 November–1 December 2026
- **Location:** Yamoussoukro, Côte d’Ivoire
- **Instructors:** Wim Simonse and Hyacinthe Ouattara
- **Fee:** USD 800 (or CFA 400,000) per participant

The second course examines carbon credits as a potential source of green finance for the cashew sector. Such opportunities require careful assessment of eligibility, measurement, verification and practical feasibility; the announcement does not promise funding to participants.

According to ACA, each course fee includes tuition, accommodation, meals and certification. Applicants may express interest in one or both courses.

## Relevance for Cambodia

For Cambodian cashew growers, processors, exporters and industry support organizations, the announcement offers a useful international example of how professional training can connect market intelligence with sustainability finance. Better market information may support commercial planning, while understanding carbon-credit mechanisms may help stakeholders evaluate future possibilities. These are potential lessons, not confirmed Cambodian partnerships or benefits.

## Applications and source

ACA directs interested applicants to **https://masterclass.cashewknowledgehub.com/** or **ysore@africancashewalliance.com** for further details. Readers should confirm eligibility, availability and current arrangements with the organizer before making travel or payment commitments.
