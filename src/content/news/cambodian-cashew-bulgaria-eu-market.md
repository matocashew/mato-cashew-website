A Cambodian business delegation led by **Pinex Agro Co., Ltd.** recently completed a trade and market study mission to Latvia, Romania, and Bulgaria, with the aim of exploring new opportunities for Cambodian agricultural products in European markets.

The mission examined market demand, logistics, packaging requirements, investment opportunities, and potential trade partnerships. In Bulgaria, the delegation engaged with government agencies, business organisations, municipalities, food producers, and distributors to explore practical opportunities for connecting Cambodian products with Bulgarian and wider European markets.

## Cambodian Cashews and European Market Opportunities

A key focus of the discussions was Cambodia's growing cashew sector. The delegation presented the potential of the Cambodian cashew value chain, covering cultivation, harvesting, drying, processing, packaging, branding, and international distribution.

Particular attention was given to increasing domestic processing and developing higher-value products rather than relying mainly on exports of raw agricultural materials.

During discussions with Bulgarian food distributor **FAMILEX**, Cambodian representatives presented opportunities for both raw and processed cashew products. According to the source, the parties discussed using cashews as a pilot product to assess market demand, price competitiveness, EU food-safety requirements, and potential retail distribution in Bulgaria.

## Bulgaria as a Gateway to the EU Market

The delegation also explored Bulgaria's potential role as a regional processing, repacking, and distribution gateway for Cambodian agricultural products entering the Balkan region and the wider European Union market.

Discussions included food-safety compliance, quality control, packaging and labelling requirements, logistics, and product adaptation for European consumers.

Further discussions with Bulgarian food manufacturer **DOMA** considered opportunities to integrate Cambodian cashews into confectionery and bakery products, as well as possibilities for direct sourcing, processing technology, packaging, workforce development, and supplier cooperation.

## Business Cooperation and Market Development

The mission also resulted in a Memorandum of Understanding between **Pinex Agro Co., Ltd.** and **M3 Group**, establishing a framework for marketing, public relations, business matching, and investment support between Cambodian and Bulgarian businesses.

For Cambodia's cashew industry, these discussions highlight opportunities beyond raw cashew exports. Greater attention to processing, food safety, packaging, product development, branding, and international distribution could support higher value addition in Cambodia while creating new pathways to European markets.
