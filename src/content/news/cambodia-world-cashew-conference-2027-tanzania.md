Cambodia's cashew sector is preparing to strengthen its international connections through participation in the **World Cashew Conference 2027** in Dar es Salaam, Tanzania.

According to information published by the **Cashew Nut Association of Cambodia (CAC)**, the association plans to send **two representatives** to participate in the international conference. The participation provides an opportunity for Cambodia's cashew sector to build relationships with industry stakeholders, exchange knowledge, and learn from developments in other major cashew-producing regions.

## World Cashew Conference 2027

The World Cashew Conference 2027 is scheduled to take place from **21 to 23 January 2027** at the **Julius Nyerere International Convention Centre in Dar es Salaam, Tanzania**.

The conference is expected to bring together participants from across the global cashew value chain, including producers, processors, exporters, buyers, industry associations, technology providers, and other sector partners.

For Cambodia, participation in an international industry platform can provide useful opportunities to understand market developments, processing technologies, quality requirements, and approaches being used in other cashew-producing countries.

## Strengthening International Relationships

CAC indicated that participation in the conference is intended not only to gather industry knowledge but also to strengthen international relationships.

CAC Chairman **Mr. Silot Uon** is expected to use the visit as an opportunity to strengthen an existing relationship with a professor at Zanzibar University.

Such connections can support further exchange of knowledge and may create opportunities for future cooperation between Cambodian and Tanzanian stakeholders, particularly in agriculture and the cashew sector.

## Learning from East Africa's Cashew Industry

Tanzania is an important cashew-producing country in East Africa and has long experience in cashew cultivation, trade, and industry development.

Engagement with stakeholders from Tanzania and other participating countries may allow Cambodian representatives to learn from different approaches to production, processing, market development, and value-chain management.

International knowledge exchange is increasingly relevant as Cambodia works to expand domestic cashew processing and increase the value generated from cashew production within the country.

## Opportunities for Cambodia's Cashew Sector

Cambodia is one of the world's significant producers of raw cashew nuts. Strengthening connections with international industry participants can support the country's efforts to develop a more competitive domestic cashew value chain.

Participation in global industry events can contribute to several areas, including:

- international market connections;
- knowledge of processing technology;
- understanding international quality requirements;
- exchange of production and processing experience;
- cooperation with industry associations and research institutions; and
- greater international visibility for Cambodian cashews.

These opportunities can complement Cambodia's broader efforts to increase domestic processing, improve product quality, and develop stronger links with international markets.

## Mato Cashew Perspective

For **Mato Cashew**, stronger international engagement in the cashew sector is a positive development for Cambodia.

Knowledge exchange, market connections, research cooperation, and exposure to international processing practices can provide useful lessons for farmers, processors, businesses, and other stakeholders throughout Cambodia's cashew value chain.

Participation in events such as the World Cashew Conference can also help increase international awareness of Cambodia's cashew industry and its potential to develop higher-value processed products.
