## PINEX AGRO Signs Cooperation Agreement with Canadian Institutions



Cambodian agricultural company PINEX AGRO signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with Canada's Centre for Climate Change Research (CCCR) and A.R. Environmental Solutions Inc. (ARES) on 15 November 2026 during FrancoTech 2026 in Phnom Penh.



According to the Cashew Nut Association of Cambodia (CAC), the agreement was signed by PINEX AGRO Chairwoman Mrs. Pin Phiroum and Dr. Azizur Rahman, President and Chief Executive Officer of CCCR and ARES.



The partnership is intended to strengthen agricultural trade, technology exchange and sustainable farming cooperation between Cambodia and Canada.



## New Market Opportunities for Cambodian Cashews



A major focus of the cooperation is expanding opportunities in Canada for Cambodian raw cashew nuts and processed cashew products. The agreement also covers fresh and dried mangoes, alongside the potential import of Canadian agricultural products such as organic cherries, bell peppers and fertilizers.



The initiative could help Cambodian agricultural businesses develop new international buyer relationships and explore opportunities beyond existing markets.



CAC President Silot Uon welcomed the cooperation, highlighting its potential to improve access for Cambodian agricultural products, particularly cashews, to Canadian and wider North American markets.

## Agricultural Technology and Quality Improvement



Beyond trade, the partnership seeks to encourage the exchange of agricultural knowledge and technologies. Proposed cooperation includes capacity-building activities related to crop productivity, product quality, climate resilience and sustainable farming practices.



These areas are relevant to Cambodia's cashew sector, where consistent raw material quality, efficient processing and compliance with buyer requirements are important for developing export opportunities.



The MoU establishes a framework for cooperation; specific technology transfers, training programs and measurable results will depend on subsequent implementation.



## Coordination with Five Cambodian Agricultural Companies



PINEX AGRO is expected to serve as the lead Cambodian partner under the Cambodia–Canada Partnership Program and coordinate with five selected Cambodian farmers' companies.



The planned coordination covers agricultural sourcing, processing, quality control and exports. It could create opportunities to connect producers and processors with international market requirements.



The CAC announcement does not identify the five companies or confirm individual export contracts arising from the arrangement.



## Understanding the Five-Year Trade Target



The MoU sets out an indicative bilateral agricultural trade target of CAD 1.45 billion over five years.



This amount is a preliminary planning estimate covering broader agricultural trade between the two countries. It is not a confirmed investment, guaranteed sales agreement or forecast of cashew exports alone.



Actual commercial results will depend on future negotiations, market demand, regulatory requirements, financing and implementation by the participating organizations.



## Outlook for Cambodia's Cashew Industry



PINEX AGRO is based in Kampong Thom Province and is involved in sourcing, processing and supplying agricultural products, particularly Cambodian cashew nuts.



If the cooperation leads to practical business arrangements, improved quality systems and market access, it could support Cambodia's efforts to increase the value of domestically processed cashew products.



For now, the agreement represents an early stage of international cooperation. Future developments will determine its contribution to Cambodia's cashew trade and agricultural technology capacity.
