# The Complete Guide to Cashew Processing

Cashew processing transforms raw cashew nuts into safe, premium-quality kernels ready for domestic consumption and international export. Every stage—from harvesting to final packaging—requires careful control to preserve quality, food safety, and kernel value.

<InfoBox title="🏭 Why Processing Matters" variant="tip">

Professional processing protects kernel quality, improves food safety, and maximizes export value while reducing waste.

</InfoBox>

## Processing Overview

| Stage | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Harvesting | Collect mature nuts |
| Drying | Reduce moisture |
| Storage | Preserve quality |
| Cleaning | Remove foreign materials |
| Steaming | Prepare shells |
| Shelling | Remove hard shell |
| Drying Kernels | Improve peeling |
| Peeling | Remove testa |
| Grading | Sort by size and quality |
| Quality Inspection | Verify export standards |
| Packaging | Maintain freshness |
| Export | Deliver to global buyers |

## 1. Harvesting

Only naturally fallen mature cashew apples are collected. The nut is separated from the apple and prepared for drying.

## 2. Drying

Raw nuts are sun-dried until safe moisture levels are reached for storage and processing.

<InfoBox title="☀️ Drying Tip" variant="tip">

Uniform drying helps reduce mold risk and improves storage stability.

</InfoBox>

## 3. Storage

Nuts are stored in clean, dry, well-ventilated warehouses before processing.

## 4. Cleaning

Foreign materials, stones, dust, and damaged nuts are removed before thermal treatment.

## 5. Steaming

Steam softens the shell, making shell removal safer while reducing exposure to Cashew Nut Shell Liquid (CNSL).

<InfoBox title="⚠️ Safety" variant="warning">

Raw shells contain CNSL, which can irritate skin. Proper equipment and protective measures are essential.

</InfoBox>

## 6. Shelling

Mechanical or manual shelling separates the kernel from the shell while minimizing breakage.

## 7. Kernel Drying

Shelled kernels are dried to loosen the testa (thin skin).

## 8. Peeling

Workers or specialized equipment remove the testa carefully to maintain whole kernels.

Grading follows internationally recognized standards based on size, color, shape, and defects.

## 10. Quality Inspection

Quality inspectors verify:

- Moisture
- Color
- Size
- Defects
- Foreign matter
- Food safety

## 11. Packaging

Vacuum packaging preserves freshness and extends shelf life during international transportation.

## 12. Cambodia Perspective

Cambodia is rapidly expanding domestic processing capacity to increase value-added exports and strengthen the local cashew industry.

<InfoBox title="🇰🇭 Cambodia Insight" variant="insight">

Expanding local processing creates jobs, improves traceability, and increases export competitiveness.

</InfoBox>

## Key Takeaways

- Cashew processing consists of multiple controlled stages.
- Food safety and quality control are essential throughout production.
- Proper grading increases export value.
- Modern packaging protects kernel quality.
- Domestic processing strengthens Cambodia's agricultural economy.
