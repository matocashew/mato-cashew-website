Cashew kernels are graded according to internationally recognized standards based on their size, color, and condition.

## Whole Kernels

Whole kernels are the most valuable grade because they remain intact after processing.

## Split Kernels

Split kernels are separated into halves but still maintain excellent eating quality.

## Broken Pieces

Broken kernels are widely used in snacks, confectionery, and food manufacturing.

## Color Classification

Premium kernels should have a uniform ivory or light cream color without discoloration.

## Moisture Control

Proper moisture content helps maintain freshness, texture, and shelf life.

## Export Standards

Before shipment, kernels are inspected to ensure they meet international food safety and quality requirements.
