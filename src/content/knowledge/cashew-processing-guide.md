Cashew processing transforms raw cashew nuts into high-quality edible kernels through several carefully controlled stages.

## Harvesting

Fresh cashew nuts are collected after naturally falling from the tree.

## Drying

The nuts are dried to reduce moisture before storage and processing.

## Roasting

Roasting helps loosen the hard shell while preserving kernel quality.

## Shelling

Machines and skilled workers remove the shell carefully to avoid damaging the kernel.

## Peeling

The thin skin covering the kernel is removed using heat and manual inspection.

## Grading

Finished kernels are graded according to international quality standards.

## Packaging

Premium kernels are vacuum packed to maintain freshness before shipment.
