# Cashew Quality Standards

Cashew quality standards define the characteristics that premium kernels should meet before they are sold in domestic or international markets. These standards help ensure that buyers receive products that are safe, consistent, and suitable for their intended applications.

Unlike grading, which classifies kernels by size, shape, and color, quality standards evaluate the overall condition of the kernels after processing.

<InfoBox
  type="info"
  title="Quality vs. Grading"
>

Grading identifies **what a kernel looks like**, while quality standards evaluate **how well the kernel meets industry requirements**.

A premium grade can still fail quality inspection if defects, contamination, or excessive moisture are present.

</InfoBox>

## Main Quality Factors

International buyers assess several important characteristics during quality inspection.

### Appearance

Premium kernels should have a clean, natural appearance with minimal surface damage and a uniform shape.

### Color

Kernel color should be consistent within the selected grade. Excessive discoloration may indicate processing problems or storage issues.

### Size Uniformity

Kernels within the same package should have a consistent size according to the selected grade.

### Moisture Content

Proper moisture helps maintain freshness, texture, and shelf life while reducing the risk of mold growth during storage.

### Defects

Inspectors evaluate kernels for defects such as discoloration, insect damage, mold, broken pieces, and foreign materials.

### Cleanliness

Finished products should be free from shells, dust, stones, metal fragments, insects, or other contaminants.

## Food Safety Requirements

In addition to physical quality, international buyers expect cashew kernels to meet food safety requirements.

Important food safety practices include:

- Maintaining hygienic processing facilities.
- Preventing cross-contamination.
- Using clean packaging materials.
- Controlling pests throughout storage.
- Following documented quality control procedures.

Food safety requirements complement grading and quality standards by ensuring that products are safe for consumers as well as visually appealing.
---

## Quality Inspection Process

Quality inspection is performed throughout the production process to ensure that cashew kernels consistently meet buyer specifications and international standards.

The typical inspection process includes:

1. Receiving raw kernels.
2. Visual inspection for defects.
3. Moisture testing.
4. Sorting by quality and grade.
5. Removal of defective kernels.
6. Packaging under hygienic conditions.
7. Final quality verification before shipment.

<InfoBox
  type="success"
  title="Continuous Quality Control"
>

Quality inspection is not a single step. It is a continuous process performed throughout production to maintain consistency and reduce the risk of export rejection.

</InfoBox>
