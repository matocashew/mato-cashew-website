### 4. Cashew Moisture Specifications

#### 4.1 AFI
- AFI Specifications for Cashew Kernels
- 3%–5% moisture
- adopted September 2023
- measurement method reference

#### 4.2 UNECE DDP-17
- Cashew Kernels standard
- 2023 edition
- maximum moisture content 5.0%
- commercial and marketing quality context

#### 4.3 How the references differ
Central message:
AFI and UNECE broadly converge near a 5% upper level but should not be presented as one identical or universal requirement.

### 6. Wet Basis vs Dry Basis
Keep concise.

#### Wet basis
Water as a share of total sample mass.

#### Dry basis
Water relative to dry solids.

Key message:
Always check the basis before comparing research values or process measurements.

No dedicated component required unless readability testing shows a need.

### 10. How to Interpret Moisture Results
Distinguish:
- commercial specification result;
- internal process-monitoring result;
- buyer/contract requirement;
- research measurement.

Key message:
A moisture number has meaning only when its **product stage, method, basis, and specification context** are known.

### 11. Common Moisture-Control Mistakes
Potential items:

- comparing raw-nut values with finished kernels;
