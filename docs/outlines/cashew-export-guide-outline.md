## Working title
**Cashew Export Guide: From Product Readiness to International Shipment**

## Article role
A technical-to-commercial bridge that explains export readiness as a process framework rather than a universal legal checklist.

## Primary reader question
**What should a cashew exporter verify and prepare from product readiness through shipment, and which requirements depend on the buyer, commodity, origin, and destination?**

### 17. Pre-Shipment Verification Framework
Use a process-oriented checklist:
1. Product defined.
2. Buyer specs confirmed.
3. Quality / safety confirmed.
4. Packaging / marking checked.
5. Export-side documents ready.
6. Origin requirements checked.
7. Plant-health requirements checked.
8. Destination requirements verified.
9. Logistics arranged.
10. Traceability records consistent.

**Important:** Label as a verification framework, not universal legal checklist.

### 18. Common Export Mistakes
Potential examples:
- treating one document list as universal;
- confusing RCN and kernel requirements;
- assuming phytosanitary certificate is always required;
- assuming C/O is always identical;
- ignoring importer responsibilities;
- failing to verify current destination rules;
- mismatched lot / invoice / packing data;
- shipment with damaged packaging;
- relying on an outdated web article.
