# Cashew Export Guide — Asset Plan

## Sprint
Sprint 14

## Asset strategy
Use visuals to explain export readiness and shipment flow without embedding fixed regulatory requirements that can become stale.

## Asset 1 — Hero Banner
**Status: Required**

### Purpose
Represent finished cashew kernels prepared for international commercial shipment.

### Visual direction
Possible elements:
- premium processed cashew kernels;
- professional export packaging;
- labeled cartons / sealed packages;
- port / container / freight context;
- subtle world-trade visual language;
- quality-control or documentation cues.

### Visual message
**From product readiness to international shipment.**

### Avoid
- fixed “mandatory document” lists;
- fake customs stamps;
- fake certificates;
- country flags implying universal legal rules;
- tariff percentages;
- “guaranteed export compliance” claims;
- cluttered global-map graphics.

### Suggested neutral text
- `Cashew Export Guide`
- `From Product Readiness to International Shipment`

### Likely filename
`cashew-export-guide.jpg`

### Alt-text intent
`Processed cashew kernels in professional export packaging with international shipment and logistics context.`

---

## Asset 2 — Export Readiness Flow Diagram
**Status: Recommended**

### Flow
Define Product  
→ Confirm Buyer Specs  
→ Quality & Food Safety  
→ Packaging & Marking  
→ Export-Side Documents  
→ Origin / Phytosanitary Check  
→ Destination Requirements  
→ Logistics / Customs  
→ Shipment / Traceability

### Important note
This is a **conceptual verification framework**, not a universal legal checklist.

### Caption
“Conceptual cashew export-readiness flow. Actual documents, certificates, filings, and responsibilities depend on the product, origin, buyer, destination, shipment, and current requirements.”

### Likely filename
`cashew-export-readiness-flow.jpg`

### Design requirements
- readable on mobile;
- no fixed document names presented as universally mandatory;
- no legal guarantee language;
- consistent with Mato Cashew Knowledge visual language.

## Explicitly do not create
- “10 mandatory export documents” infographic
- tariff table image
- phytosanitary-is-always-required badge
- Certificate-of-Origin-is-always-required badge
- country-by-country legal checklist graphic
- fake customs / government certification marks

## Accessibility
- meaningful alt text;
- essential compliance concepts remain in HTML;
- diagram labels readable on mobile;
- no important safeguard exists only inside an image.
