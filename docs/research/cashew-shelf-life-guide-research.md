# Cashew Shelf Life Guide — Research Notes

## Sprint
Sprint 13

## Core research conclusion
Shelf life is a **system outcome**, not one universal number:

Initial product condition + moisture/water activity + packaging/seal integrity + storage environment + time → quality change → acceptance/specification endpoint → shelf-life performance.

## Evidence boundaries

### Product form
Do not transfer shelf-life findings automatically between:
- raw cashew nuts (RCN);
- processed/unroasted kernels;
- roasted kernels;
- flavoured/formulated products.

### Storage environment
Cashew-specific literature supports temperature, moisture, light and storage atmosphere as factors influencing quality change. RCN evidence must remain labelled as RCN evidence.

### Packaging
Packaging influences moisture exchange, oxygen exposure, contamination protection, physical protection and seal integrity, but packaging alone does not determine shelf life.

A study observation period (including a 12-month study) is **not** automatically a validated shelf-life claim.

### Oxidation
Lipid oxidation is an important quality-deterioration mechanism. Chemical indicators may track change, while sensory outcomes can include off-aroma and off-flavour. No single indicator automatically defines the complete shelf-life endpoint.

### Shelf-life endpoint
Potential dimensions include:
- Chemical: oxidative/peroxide-related change
- Physical: moisture, water activity, texture, hardness, colour
- Sensory: aroma, flavour, acceptability
- Microbiological: microbial/fungal condition where relevant
- Package/pest: seal integrity, damage, infestation

### Quality vs food safety
Quality shelf life is not the same as food-safety validation. Broader hazard-control content belongs in the Cashew Food Safety article.

### Real-time testing
Define product → package → storage conditions → acceptance criteria → sampling/testing → track change → determine validated endpoint.

### Accelerated testing
Accelerated studies use stress conditions to produce change faster. Accelerated test duration cannot automatically be converted into real-world months without an appropriate validated model.

### Refrigeration/freezing
Current Sprint research does not support a universal cashew-kernel refrigerated/frozen duration table. Temperature may be discussed as a deterioration-rate factor; exact commercial durations require product-specific validation.

### Best-before/date marking
Shelf-life study → evidence for a defined system → validated shelf-life decision → commercial date marking + applicable market requirements.

Do not imply that a processor can copy a duration from a general article or research paper directly onto a product label.

## Factual safeguards
- [ ] No universal X-month/X-year shelf-life claim.
- [ ] RCN ≠ processed kernels ≠ roasted ≠ flavoured products.
- [ ] Study duration ≠ validated shelf life.
- [ ] Packaging/vacuum alone ≠ shelf life.
- [ ] No unsupported refrigeration/freezing duration.
- [ ] Accelerated testing ≠ real-time duration without validated modelling.
- [ ] Quality shelf life ≠ food-safety validation.
- [ ] Shelf-life evidence ≠ automatic best-before/date marking.
- [ ] General nut and cashew-oil evidence retain their scopes.
- [ ] Existing Moisture, Packaging, Storage, Quality and Food Safety articles are linked rather than duplicated.

## Research Gate
**PASS — for article development, not for declaring a universal duration.**
