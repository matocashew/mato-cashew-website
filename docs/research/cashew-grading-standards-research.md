## What is Cashew Grading?

Status:
Verified

Sources:

- AFI Specifications for Cashew Kernels
- ComCashew Processing Guide

Notes:

Cashew grading is the internationally recognized system used to classify processed cashew kernels according to:

- Size
- Color
- Shape
- Quality
- Defects

The grading system helps buyers and sellers communicate product specifications consistently across international markets.

Cashew grades are primarily used after shelling, peeling, drying, and quality inspection.

Importance

- Standardizes international trade
- Determines commercial value
- Helps buyers select the correct product
- Ensures consistent quality

## Moisture

Status:
Verified

Standard

3–5%

Notes

Moisture is one of the most important quality indicators.

Excess moisture may:

- Reduce shelf life
- Cause blocking
- Increase mold risk

Low moisture helps maintain quality during storage and export.
