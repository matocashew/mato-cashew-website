# Article Writing Template

## 1. Hero

- Title
- Description
- Hero Image

---

## 2. Introduction

Explain the topic in 2–4 paragraphs.

---

## 3. Quick Facts

Provide a summary table when appropriate.

---

## 4. Main Sections

Use logical H2 headings.

Examples:

- Definition
- Types
- Process
- Benefits
- Applications

---

## 5. Info Boxes

Use InfoBox for:

- Tips
- Insights
- Important Notes

---

## 6. Tables

Use ComparisonTable whenever information is easier to compare.

---

## 7. Cambodia Perspective

Explain the topic in the context of Cambodia whenever applicable.

---

## 8. Key Takeaways

Summarize the article in bullet points.

---

## 9. FAQ

Answer common questions.

---

## 10. Related Articles

Internal links to related knowledge articles.
