# Mato Cashew Knowledge Center Editorial Guide

## Purpose

The Mato Cashew Knowledge Center is designed to become a trusted educational resource about Cambodian cashews.

Every article should educate readers first while naturally introducing Mato Cashew's expertise.

---

# Standard Article Structure

1. Hero
2. Quick Facts
3. Introduction
4. Table of Contents
5. Main Content
6. Did You Know?
7. Cambodia Perspective
8. Comparison Table (if applicable)
9. Key Takeaways
10. Frequently Asked Questions
11. Related Articles

---

# Writing Principles

- Write for humans first.
- Use clear English.
- Avoid unnecessary marketing language.
- Explain technical terms.
- Use short paragraphs.
- Support statements with reliable sources during research.
- Add Cambodia-specific context whenever possible.

---

# Heading Rules

- One H1 only.
- Use H2 for major sections.
- Use H3 for subsections.
- Avoid skipping heading levels.

---

# Images

- Use meaningful images.
- Every image must include descriptive alt text.
- Prefer original Mato Cashew photography whenever available.

---

# Internal Linking

Each article should link naturally to related knowledge articles.

Example:

- What is Cashew?
- Cashew Processing Guide
- Cashew Grading Standards

---

# Knowledge Blocks

Use reusable UI components whenever appropriate:

- InfoBox
- FAQ
- ComparisonTable

---

# Tone

Professional

Educational

Friendly

Trustworthy

Neutral
---

# Research & Sources Policy

Every article should be based on reliable and verifiable information.

Preferred sources:

1. Academic publications
2. Government agencies
3. International organizations (e.g. FAO)
4. Scientific institutions
5. Industry associations
6. Mato Cashew's own verified operational experience

Avoid:

- Anonymous blogs
- AI-generated content without verification
- Marketing claims without evidence
- Unverified statistics

When Cambodia-specific information is available, prioritize official Cambodian sources or internationally recognized publications covering Cambodia.
